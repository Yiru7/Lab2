Film & Media Projects

A small collection of creative projects exploring film, visual design, and digital media. Each project focuses on a different way of communicating ideas through images, storytelling, and interactive design.

Short Film Concept

A short film project focused on atmosphere, visual storytelling, and suspense. The project explores how framing, lighting, and pacing can shape the emotional experience of a scene.

Visual Identity

A visual design project combining typography, color, and graphic composition to create a consistent identity. The goal was to communicate a clear mood while keeping the design simple and recognizable.

Interactive Web Piece

A web-based project exploring responsive layouts and digital interaction. The design focuses on clear information hierarchy, accessibility, and creating a comfortable experience across different screen sizes.
